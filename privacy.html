what stays on your computer

the dashboard saves your niche keywords, duration, pacing and engagement settings in your browser’s local site storage. the extension temporarily stores session settings, tab identifiers, action counts, activity messages and comment history in Chrome’s session storage. comment history includes the exact submitted text, post link, author, time and whether submission was confirmed. it remains available in the panel and connected dashboard after the session ends; starting another session replaces it. temporary session storage clears when Chrome closes.

the extension reads visible instagram content and controls to identify matching posts and check whether an action succeeded. this information is processed locally. our code does not upload captions, existing instagram credentials, cookies, session activity or warm-up settings to our server.

account creation and phone verification

account creation is available in the extension side panel. your signup password stays in trusted temporary extension storage and is entered only into the selected platform’s signup page. it is cleared when signup stops or finishes. the generated email, platform, username, configured birthday, mailbox access key and recovery progress remain in extension-local storage. our existing account email service holds generated addresses, received verification emails and saved usernames.

if you connect smspool, its secret key is stored only in trusted extension session storage and clears when chrome closes or you disconnect. the key is sent directly to smspool over https; it is not sent to our website, our email service, the platform page or persistent storage. the extension requests temporary number prices, orders the selected number when needed, checks activation and reads the code for that order. smspool processes these requests under its own privacy policy.

the order id, phone number, recorded expiry, provider credential fingerprint and phone signup progress stay in extension-local storage for recovery. verification codes are used for the active signup and are not included in persistent account history. message identifiers are retained to avoid reusing old codes. the website and platform pages cannot request the smspool key or read the extension’s account history.

permissions

instagram and tiktok access lets the extension inspect visible posts, scroll and interact with controls in the tab you choose. scripting runs the extension’s bundled code in those selected tabs. storage keeps track of your current session and saved signup recovery. access to our account email service enables signup email preparation and code retrieval; smspool access enables the temporary phone verification workflow. a small connection script runs only on our warm-up website so its controls can communicate with the extension.

what goes to other services

actions included in the session amounts you start are sent to Instagram through your signed-in browser, just as interactions on that site are. the dashboard is hosted on Vercel, which may process ordinary connection and hosting logs, such as your IP address and requested pages. extension downloads are served from the website. we have not added analytics, advertising trackers or a login service.

your choices

likes, follows and comments have visible automatic amounts based on session length and pacing. you can edit each amount; set it to 0 to turn that action off. starting a session authorizes actions within those limits. stop at any time. stop does not cancel or refund a temporary phone order. each order has a maximum price and no renewal. manage refunds and existing orders in smspool. removing the extension clears its storage and its private signup-email access key; keep your assigned email details before removing it. do not include the access key in public messages. clearing this website’s site data removes dashboard preferences. stopping does not undo actions already sent to Instagram.
