what stays on your computer

the dashboard saves your niche keywords, duration, pacing and engagement settings in your browser’s local site storage. the extension temporarily stores session settings, tab identifiers, action counts and activity messages in Chrome’s session storage. temporary session storage clears when Chrome closes.

the extension reads visible instagram content and controls to identify matching posts and check whether an action succeeded. this information is processed locally. our code does not upload captions, existing instagram credentials, cookies, session activity or warm-up settings to our server.

optional account signup

the extension prepares a private signup email through our existing email service without connecting a student dashboard. a random access key is generated inside your extension and retained in its local storage, together with signup email, platform, username and configured birthday records. that key is sent only to our email service to prepare or recover this installation’s mailboxes and read matching verification codes. the service stores the generated addresses, received verification emails and saved usernames under our team account. other student mailboxes are not exposed to the extension.

your new account password is held temporarily in Chrome session storage and filled only into the selected platform’s signup form. it is cleared from the panel after starting and from the active signup when you stop, finish, close its platform or runner tab, or the session expiry is next checked. Chrome session storage clears on browser restart. the password is never sent to our email service or public website. the extension can submit recognised signup steps and email codes, including the configured birthday on supported instagram signup forms. this beta defaults to may 30, 2006. the birthday is filled into instagram and saved in local account records, not sent to our email service. unsupported birthday controls, phone checks, security checks and unclear forms pause for you. stopping cannot undo an action already sent or remove fields already entered on the platform.

permissions

instagram access lets the extension inspect visible posts, scroll and interact with controls in the tab you choose. tiktok access supports its signup fields. access to our website’s email endpoint supports private signup mailboxes and verification codes without a dashboard tab. scripting runs the extension’s bundled code in those selected tabs. storage keeps track of your current session. a small connection script runs only on our warm-up website so its controls can communicate with the extension.

what goes to other services

actions you choose to enable are sent to Instagram through your signed-in browser, just as interactions on that site are. the dashboard is hosted on Vercel, which may process ordinary connection and hosting logs, such as your IP address and requested pages. extension downloads are served from the website. we have not added analytics, advertising trackers or a login service.

your choices

comments are off unless you turn them on. adjust your limits before starting; stop at any time. removing the extension clears its storage and its private signup-email access key; keep your assigned email details before removing it. do not include the access key in public messages. and clearing this website’s site data removes dashboard preferences. stopping does not undo actions already sent to Instagram.
