what stays on your computer

the dashboard saves your niche keywords, duration, pacing and engagement settings in your browser’s local site storage. the extension temporarily stores session settings, tab identifiers, action counts and activity messages in Chrome’s session storage. temporary session storage clears when Chrome closes.

the extension reads visible instagram content and controls to identify matching posts and check whether an action succeeded. this information is processed locally. our code does not upload captions, existing instagram credentials, cookies, session activity or warm-up settings to our server.

optional account signup

the extension side panel can connect to the student dashboard tab you choose. it reads your signed-in profile name, your account email addresses and their saved usernames. when requested, it generates an email, reads a fresh verification code for that email and platform, or saves the username you confirm. these requests use the dashboard’s existing sign-in. the extension never reads or exports dashboard cookies or sign-in tokens.

your new account password is held temporarily in Chrome session storage to fill the platform’s signup form. it is cleared from the panel after starting and from the active signup when you stop, confirm completion, close a connected tab, or the session expiry is next checked. it is not saved to the student dashboard or the public warm-up website. Chrome session storage clears on browser restart. signup details and email codes are entered only into the selected instagram or tiktok tab, where the platform can read them as soon as they are filled. stopping cannot remove fields already entered on the platform.

permissions

instagram access lets the extension inspect visible posts, scroll and interact with controls in the tab you choose. tiktok access supports its signup fields. student dashboard access connects your own account emails to the signup helper. scripting runs the extension’s bundled code in those selected tabs. storage keeps track of your current session. a small connection script runs only on our warm-up website so its controls can communicate with the extension.

what goes to other services

actions you choose to enable are sent to Instagram through your signed-in browser, just as interactions on that site are. the dashboard is hosted on Vercel, which may process ordinary connection and hosting logs, such as your IP address and requested pages. extension downloads are served from the website. we have not added analytics, advertising trackers or a login service.

your choices

comments are off unless you turn them on. adjust your limits before starting; stop at any time. removing the extension clears its storage, and clearing this website’s site data removes dashboard preferences. stopping does not undo actions already sent to Instagram.
