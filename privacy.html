your browser, your session.

last updated september 8, 2026.

what stays on your computer

the dashboard saves your niche keywords, duration, pacing and engagement settings in your browser’s local site storage. the extension temporarily stores session settings, tab identifiers, action counts and activity messages in Chrome’s session storage. temporary session storage clears when Chrome closes.

the extension reads visible instagram content and controls to identify matching posts and check whether an action succeeded. this information is processed locally. our code does not upload captions, existing instagram credentials, cookies, session activity or warm-up settings to our server.

previous account signup records

account creation is paused in version 0.6.9. this release does not request signup passwords, create mailboxes or retrieve verification codes. an earlier active signup is stopped and its temporary password is cleared when the updated extension starts. this cannot undo information already entered or actions already sent to the platform.

if you used an earlier signup release, its generated email, platform, username, configured birthday and private mailbox access key remain in extension-local storage. the existing email service may still hold the previously generated addresses, received verification emails and saved usernames. these records are not deleted by this update.

permissions

instagram access lets the extension inspect visible posts, scroll and interact with controls in the tab you choose. scripting runs the extension’s bundled code in those selected tabs. storage keeps track of your current session. a small connection script runs only on our warm-up website so its controls can communicate with the extension.

what goes to other services

actions included in the session amounts you start are sent to Instagram through your signed-in browser, just as interactions on that site are. the dashboard is hosted on Vercel, which may process ordinary connection and hosting logs, such as your IP address and requested pages. extension downloads are served from the website. we have not added analytics, advertising trackers or a login service.

your choices

likes, follows and comments have visible automatic amounts based on session length and pacing. you can edit each amount; set it to 0 to turn that action off. starting a session authorizes actions within those limits. stop at any time. removing the extension clears its storage and its private signup-email access key; keep your assigned email details before removing it. do not include the access key in public messages. clearing this website’s site data removes dashboard preferences. stopping does not undo actions already sent to Instagram.
