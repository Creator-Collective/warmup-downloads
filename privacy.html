what stays on your computer

the dashboard saves your niche keywords, duration, pacing and engagement settings in your browser’s local site storage. the extension temporarily stores session settings, tab identifiers, action counts, activity messages and comment history in Chrome’s session storage. comment history includes the exact submitted text, post link, author, time and whether submission was confirmed. it remains available in the panel and connected dashboard after the session ends; starting another session replaces it. temporary session storage clears when Chrome closes.

the extension reads visible instagram content and controls to identify matching posts and check whether an action succeeded. this information is processed locally. our code does not upload captions, existing instagram credentials, cookies, session activity or warm-up settings to our server.

saved data from earlier versions

account creation and phone setup are disabled in this version. the extension does not create mailboxes, buy phone numbers or retrieve verification codes. updating stops unfinished signup sessions and clears their temporary password. saved emails, mailbox access and phone-order history from earlier versions remain on your device. updating does not cancel or refund an earlier phone order; manage those directly in smspool.

permissions

instagram and tiktok access lets the extension inspect visible posts, scroll and interact with controls in the tab you choose. scripting runs the extension’s bundled code in those selected tabs. storage keeps track of your current session and saved signup recovery. a small connection script runs only on our warm-up website so its controls can communicate with the extension.

what goes to other services

actions included in the session amounts you start are sent to Instagram through your signed-in browser, just as interactions on that site are. the dashboard is hosted on Vercel, which may process ordinary connection and hosting logs, such as your IP address and requested pages. extension downloads are served from the website. we have not added analytics, advertising trackers or a login service.

your choices

likes, follows and comments have visible automatic amounts based on session length and pacing. you can edit each amount; set it to 0 to turn that action off. starting a session authorizes actions within those limits. stop at any time. removing the extension clears its storage and its private signup-email access key; keep your assigned email details before removing it. do not include the access key in public messages. clearing this website’s site data removes dashboard preferences. stopping does not undo actions already sent to Instagram.
