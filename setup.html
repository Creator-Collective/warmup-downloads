your first installation

  1. download and extract the zip.

    keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent, such as your documents folder.

  2. open Chrome's extensions page.

    type chrome://extensions in Chrome's address bar. turn on developer mode in the top right.

  3. choose load unpacked.

    select the extracted creator-collective-extension folder, the one containing manifest.json.

  4. pin it and open the side panel.

    click Chrome's puzzle-piece icon and pin Creator Collective Warm-up. open instagram or tiktok, then click the cc extension icon. choose your platform, tab, keywords and session length in the side panel, then press start.

manual beta installs do not update automatically. loading a different folder creates a separate copy with separate settings and signup-email access.

check your first session

set the length to 1 minute, enter your niche keywords, and set likes, follows and comments to 0. choose your platform tab and press start. you should see a search, scrolling and live activity. check that stop ends the session before enabling engagement.

while a session runs

the side panel shows live activity. a session tab also opens in the background to keep the timer running. keep that tab open, keep Chrome running and keep your computer awake. sessions run in your browser; the website does not run them on a server.

closing the side panel does not stop your session. use stop in the side panel, session tab or dashboard, or press escape in the session tab. closing Chrome, the session tab or the platform tab stops the session.

if stopping stalls, the dashboard closes the unresponsive session tab after a short wait. pressing stop again retries immediately. check the platform before restarting.

use a separate platform tab for the session. manually changing that tab's page stops it. an action already sent may still complete after you stop.

what this beta can do

search niche keywords, open a result, move through videos or posts, and try to reach the visible like, follow and comment targets on instagram or tiktok. comments use a recognized detail from a matching caption. edit any target or set it to 0 to turn that action off. it currently supports english controls and skips posts it cannot confidently read.

instagram and tiktok remember their own keywords, targets and pacing. session results show confirmed actions against your targets, with unconfirmed attempts shown separately. stopping keeps the final confirmed action.

choose auto pacing for a mix of quick skim bursts, slower watches and occasional full-video watches, plus loading time. full-video watches happen when playback can be read and there is enough time before the next keyword or session end.

add multiple keywords separated by commas. sessions rotate through them in order, usually every 2 minutes or sooner for short sessions.

this browser version is still being tested. counts show confirmed actions, not promises. store approval and testing on other clients' computers are still pending.

if the dashboard says extension needed

use regular Chrome in the same browser profile where you installed the extension. check that Creator Collective Warm-up is enabled on the extensions page, then refresh the dashboard. incognito, mobile browsers and embedded browser panels are not supported for warm-up sessions.

remove it whenever you want

open chrome://extensions and choose remove on Creator Collective Warm-up. you can clear saved dashboard settings through Chrome's site-data settings.
