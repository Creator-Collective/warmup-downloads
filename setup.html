cccreator collectiveback to warm-up

set up your browser once.

use Chrome on a mac or windows computer. the extension works with your existing instagram or tiktok login, so there is no separate desktop app.

early-access setup

the extension is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. this beta requires a manual installation. the simpler add to Chrome link will be available after store approval.

download extension zip
  1. download and extract the zip.

    keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent, such as your documents folder.

  2. open Chrome's extensions page.

    type chrome://extensions in Chrome's address bar. turn on developer mode in the top right.

  3. choose load unpacked.

    select the extracted creator-collective-extension folder, the one containing manifest.json.

  4. pin it and open the side panel.

    click Chrome's puzzle-piece icon and pin Creator Collective Warm-up. open instagram or tiktok, then click the cc extension icon. your controls appear beside the site. choose your platform, tab, keywords and session length, then press start.

open your dashboard ->

check your first session

set the length to 1 minute, enter your niche keywords, set likes and follows to 0, and set comments to 0. choose your platform tab and press start. you should see a search, scrolling and live activity. check that stop ends the session before enabling engagement.

while a session runs

the side panel shows live activity. a session tab also opens in the background to keep the timer running. keep that tab open, keep Chrome running and keep your computer awake. closing the side panel does not stop your session; press stop to end it. sessions run in your browser and stop working when Chrome closes; the website does not run them on a server. you can stop there, press escape there, or use stop on the dashboard. if stopping stalls, the dashboard closes the unresponsive session tab after a short wait; pressing stop again retries immediately. check the platform before restarting. closing the session tab or platform tab stops the session.

use a separate platform tab for the session. manually changing that tab's page stops it. an action already sent may still complete after you stop.

what this beta can do

search niche keywords, open a result, move through videos or posts, and try to reach the visible like, follow and comment targets. instagram also supports caption-based comments; tiktok comments are disabled until their live controls are verified. edit any target or set it to 0 to turn that action off. it currently supports english controls and skips posts it cannot confidently read.

this browser version is still being tested. counts show confirmed actions, not promises. store approval and testing on other clients' computers are still pending.

if the dashboard says extension needed

use regular Chrome in the same browser profile where you installed the extension. check that Creator Collective Warm-up is enabled on the extensions page, then refresh the dashboard. incognito, mobile browsers and embedded browser panels are not supported for warm-up sessions.

updating to version 0.6.26

activity now names the account you followed and whose post you liked or commented on. comments include the exact text. attempted, confirmed and unconfirmed actions stay distinct. this applies to new activity after updating; older generic messages cannot be filled in.

comments now stay in their own history above the activity feed, with the exact text, time and a link to the post. unconfirmed comments are labelled separately. history remains after the session ends or the panel reopens. starting a new session replaces it, and closing Chrome clears temporary history. earlier versions did not save comment text, so old comments cannot be recovered by this update.

likes now pace toward your target with time left for loading and confirmation. suitable posts stay open through short waits between actions, and long video watches do not delay a due like. following, commenting and scrolling remain enabled. search progress is preserved, including with a single keyword.

slow or empty searches, missing posts and brief page-loading interruptions now recover without ending warm-up. uncertain actions are not repeated or replaced with extra attempts beyond your targets. this includes the comment-draft and follow-count fixes. signing out, account restrictions, changing to a different page or pressing stop still end the session. tiktok comments stay off. account creation is available again in the extension side panel, with optional smspool phone verification.

remove it whenever you want

open chrome://extensions and choose remove on Creator Collective Warm-up. you can clear saved dashboard settings through Chrome's site-data settings.

create an account

open the extension side panel and expand “create an account”. enter your username and password. for phone verification, connect your funded smspool account in “phone setup”, then choose a monthly rental with its displayed price. the extension orders one number only when a recognized signup form needs it, saves it with your account and retrieves fresh codes. security checks and unclear forms pause for you.

phone rentals last 28–31 days depending on the option. renew your number in smspool to keep access for future logins. restarting chrome clears the smspool connection, so reconnect before using it again. stop does not cancel a purchased rental. if an order’s outcome is unclear, use its smspool rental code to recover it instead of buying another number.
