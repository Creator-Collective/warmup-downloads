cccreator collectiveback to warm-up

set up your browser once.

use Chrome on a mac or windows computer. the extension works with your existing instagram login, so there’s no separate desktop app.

early-access setup

the extension is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. this beta requires a manual installation. the simpler “add to Chrome” link will be available after store approval.

download extension zip
  1. download and extract the zip.

    keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent, such as your documents folder.

  2. open Chrome’s extensions page.

    type chrome://extensions in Chrome’s address bar. turn on “developer mode” in the top right.

  3. choose “load unpacked”.

    select the extracted creator-collective-extension folder, the one containing manifest.json.

  4. pin it and open the side panel.

    click Chrome’s puzzle-piece icon and pin Creator Collective Warm-up. open instagram, then click the cc extension icon. your controls appear beside instagram. choose your tab, keywords and session length, then press start.

open your dashboard →

check your first session

set the length to 1 minute, enter your niche keywords, set likes and follows to 0, and leave comments off. choose your instagram tab and press start. you should see a search, scrolling and live activity. check that stop ends the session before enabling engagement.

while a session runs

the side panel shows live activity. a session tab also opens in the background to keep the timer running. keep that tab open, keep Chrome running and keep your computer awake. closing the side panel does not stop your session; press stop to end it. sessions run in your browser and stop working when Chrome closes; the website does not run them on a server. you can stop there, press escape there, or use stop on the dashboard. closing the session tab or instagram tab stops the session.

use a separate instagram tab for the session. manually changing that tab’s page stops it. an action already sent may still complete after you stop.

what this beta can do

search niche keywords, open a result in instagram’s viewer, move through results with its next button, and attempt likes, follows and caption-based comments within your limits. comments are off by default. it currently supports english instagram controls and skips posts it can’t confidently read.

this browser version is still being tested. counts show confirmed actions, not promises. store approval and testing on other clients’ computers are still pending.

if the dashboard says “extension needed”

use regular Chrome in the same browser profile where you installed the extension. check that Creator Collective Warm-up is enabled on the extensions page, then refresh the dashboard. incognito, mobile browsers and embedded browser panels aren’t supported.

create an account in the side panel

open “create an account”, choose instagram or tiktok, enter your username and password, and press start. the extension prepares a signup email, opens the platform, submits recognised signup steps once, and fills a fresh email code when the platform asks for it.

if the platform asks for your birthday, phone, captcha, or a correction, finish that step in its tab and press continue in the panel. an existing login is left for you to manage. account creation and warm-up run separately; warm-up remains instagram-only.

the assigned email and username stay in this extension’s local records. your password is temporary and is never sent to our email service. keep the extension folder and storage when updating so you retain access to your signup emails. live account creation still needs testing on each platform.

updating to version 0.6.0

this update puts account creation inside the extension, with no student dashboard connection.

  1. download and unzip the latest extension above.

    keep the extracted creator-collective-extension folder. macOS may add a number to its name; that is fine.

  2. open chrome://extensions in regular Chrome.

    stop any active signup or warm-up first. to keep your saved emails and settings, replace the files inside the same permanent extension folder, then click the reload button on its existing Chrome card.

  3. check the version.

    the existing card should show version 0.6.0. if this is your first installation, use “load unpacked” and select the whole extracted folder.

  4. open the updated extension.

    click Chrome’s puzzle-piece icon, find the enabled Creator Collective Warm-up, and pin it. click its cc icon to open the side panel.

choose auto pacing for mostly 3–7 second viewing pauses, plus loading time. occasionally it stays for the remaining video time when playback can be read and there is enough time before the next keyword or session end. add multiple keywords separated by commas; sessions rotate through them in order, usually every 2 minutes or sooner for short sessions. manual beta installs do not update automatically. loading a different folder creates a separate copy with separate settings and signup-email access.

remove it whenever you want

open chrome://extensions and choose “remove” on Creator Collective Warm-up. you can clear saved dashboard settings through Chrome’s site-data settings.
