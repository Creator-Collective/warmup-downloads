from download to your first session

  1. open the app

    extract the zip and open “Creator Collective Browser.” on windows, keep the extracted folder together.

  2. use your own account

    sign into instagram inside the app. your login stays in the app on your computer.

  3. make the session yours

    add niche keywords and a duration. open session options to adjust the engagement mix and limits, then press start.

your mix, your limits

2 : 1 : 1 favors likes twice as often as follows or comments when those actions are eligible. set a share to 0 to turn it off.

leave a limit blank for automatic planning, or set your own session maximum. comments also need the auto-comments switch turned on.

the mix is a preference, not a promised result. matching content, available controls, pauses, and the session timer all affect what happens.

mac installation help

move “Creator Collective Browser” into applications, then open it. this beta is not signed with an apple developer id or notarized by apple.

if macos says the developer cannot be verified, only proceed if you trust this download and intended to install it. apple documents the per-app approval under system settings → privacy & security → open anyway. read apple’s official guide ↗

if the app is described as damaged or harmful, or your device is managed by work or school, stop and report the message. no terminal commands or changes to global security settings are needed.

what should i know before starting?

comments quote a short matching sentence from the visible caption. the app does not understand videos. comments are off by default.

press stop or escape to end a session. an action already sent cannot be undone by stopping. the app pauses for sign-in or account checks; no automation can guarantee account safety or a recommendation outcome.

this beta supports english instagram controls. live engagement and installation on other computers remain unverified.
